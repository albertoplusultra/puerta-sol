Staying near the **Puerta del Sol** means sleeping at the **exact center of Madrid**. It is the preferred choice for those who want to make the most of their visit: almost everything is just a few minutes' walk away, and the transport connections are unbeatable.

## Advantages of staying beside Sol

- **Everything at hand**: Plaza Mayor, the Royal Palace, Gran Vía and the Barrio de las Letras are just a few minutes' walk away.
- **Perfect transport**: metro and Cercanías beneath the square to get around the entire city and region.
- **Historic-center atmosphere**: experience the city by day and by night without depending on a car.
- **Ideal for New Year's Eve**: if you're coming for the chimes, staying nearby saves you long journeys.

## How to choose accommodation

Look for well-located accommodations, with good soundproofing if you are sensitive to noise, and with rooms suited to your type of trip. Booking in advance is essential in high season and, above all, for the year-end holidays.

## Accommodation in the very heart of the city

For those seeking a charming stay just meters from the square, sleeping in the historic center lets you start each day right in the heart of Madrid.
