Planning your visit to **Puerta del Sol** is simple if you keep a few things in mind. Here we gather the essentials so you can make the most of your time in the heart of Madrid.

## How to get there and get around

The square has **Sol metro station** (lines 1, 2 and 3) and **Sol commuter rail**, plus numerous bus lines. The centre is pedestrianised, so it's best to combine public transport and walking. Avoid the car due to the centre's traffic restrictions.

## How much time to spend

Seeing the square and its highlights (Kilometre Zero, the Bear and the Strawberry Tree, the Casa de Correos clock, the Tío Pepe sign) takes less than an hour. But since Sol is the ideal starting point of the historic centre, it's worth setting aside **at least half a day** to include Plaza Mayor, Mercado de San Miguel and the Royal Palace.

## Where to eat

To refuel without going far, the area offers Madrid cuisine and tapas for every taste. Dining with views over the square is a hard experience to beat.

## Where to stay

Staying next to Sol places you in the exact centre of the city, with everything on foot and excellent connections. It is especially recommended if you visit Madrid on special dates such as New Year's Eve.

## Summary for your visit

1. Arrive by metro or commuter rail at **Sol**.
2. Walk the square's highlights (Kilometre Zero, Bear and Strawberry Tree, clock, Tío Pepe).
3. Continue on foot towards **Plaza Mayor** and the **Royal Palace**.
4. Book a table and accommodation in the centre for the full experience.
