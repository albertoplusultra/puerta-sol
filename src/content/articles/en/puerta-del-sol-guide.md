**Puerta del Sol** is the heart of Madrid and the symbolic centre of Spain. This ever-bustling square brings together centuries of history, monuments every visitor wants to photograph and the city's most important transport connections. This guide gathers everything you need to understand and enjoy it.

## Why Puerta del Sol matters

Right in the historic centre, Puerta del Sol is far more than a square: it is a meeting point, the stage for historic events and the place where Spain welcomes each new year with its famous bell strokes.

Here you'll find **Kilometre Zero**, the point from which distances along the country's six radial roads are measured. It is also home to Madrid's emblem, the statue of **the Bear and the Strawberry Tree**, and the **Casa de Correos** clock, whose chimes set the rhythm of New Year's Eve.

## What to see in Puerta del Sol

- **Kilometre Zero**: the plaque on the ground where the national roads begin.
- **The Bear and the Strawberry Tree**: the city's symbol and its favourite meeting point.
- **The Casa de Correos clock**: the star of the New Year's Eve chimes.
- **The equestrian statue of Charles III**: at the centre of the square.
- **La Mariblanca**: a replica of one of the oldest sculptures linked to the square.
- **The Tío Pepe sign**: Madrid's most famous neon sign.

Each highlight has its own story, which you can explore in the articles of this guide.

## How to get there

Puerta del Sol is Madrid's most accessible transport hub. It has **Sol metro station** (lines 1, 2 and 3) and **Sol commuter rail station**, connecting with the whole region. It is a few minutes' walk from Plaza Mayor, the Royal Palace and the Barrio de las Letras.

## When to visit

The square is open 24 hours and is worth seeing both by day and by night, when lighting highlights the buildings and the Tío Pepe sign. The busiest times are weekend evenings and, of course, the night of 31 December.

## Nearby, where to eat or sleep

Puerta del Sol is the perfect base to stay and eat in central Madrid. In this guide you'll find recommendations on **where to sleep** and **where to eat** just a few metres from the square, so you can enjoy the historic centre without relying on transport.
