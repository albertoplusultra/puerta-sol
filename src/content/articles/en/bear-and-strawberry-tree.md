The statue of **the Bear and the Strawberry Tree** (El Oso y el Madroño) is probably the most recognisable image of Puerta del Sol and the **quintessential symbol of Madrid**. It depicts a bear reaching up to eat the fruit of a strawberry tree, just as it appears on the city's coat of arms.

## An emblem with centuries of history

The bear and the strawberry tree have featured in Madrid's heraldry since the Middle Ages. There are several theories about their origin: some link them to a pact between the town council and the Church, others to the abundance of bears and strawberry trees in the forests that once surrounded Madrid.

## The current statue

The bronze and stone sculpture we see today was created by the artist **Antonio Navarro Santafé** and installed in 1967. It stands about four metres tall and weighs several tonnes. Over the years it has become the city's most cited **meeting point**: "let's meet at the bear" is a common phrase among locals.

## Where to see it

It is located at the eastern end of the square, at the start of **Calle de Alcalá**. Its popularity means it is almost always surrounded by people waiting or taking photos, so be patient if you want a shot without crowds.
