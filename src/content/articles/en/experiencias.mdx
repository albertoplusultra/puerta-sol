The **Puerta del Sol** is the natural starting point for discovering Madrid. Its status as **kilometre zero** and its location in the heart of the historic quarter mean that numerous **tours, free tours and activities** begin here or pass through the square along their route.

From walking tours of Habsburg Madrid to nighttime routes of mysteries and legends, visits to the great museums or flamenco shows, these are some of the most recommended experiences that have Sol as their setting or meeting point.

## Why start at Sol

Meeting at the Puerta del Sol is easy: it is perfectly connected by metro and Cercanías, simple to find, and has unmistakable landmarks such as the Casa de Correos clock, the statue of the **Bear and the Strawberry Tree** and the **Tío Pepe** neon sign. That is why so many guides choose the square as the meeting point to begin their tours of central Madrid.
