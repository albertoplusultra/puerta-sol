Eating near the **Puerta del Sol** is one of the best ways to enjoy central Madrid. The area is packed with historic taverns, food markets and restaurants for every taste, most of them just a few minutes' walk from the square.

## What to try

Madrid's cuisine offers dishes full of character. Among the essentials are **cocido madrileño**, **callos a la madrileña**, the classic **fried calamari sandwich** and, to finish, some **churros with chocolate** or **torrijas** in season.

## Tapas in the center

The area around Sol and the neighboring Plaza Mayor are ideal for **going out for tapas**: sharing plates, local wines and a lively atmosphere. The Mercado de San Miguel, a few minutes away, is a good option for sampling a bit of everything.

## Dining with views of the Puerta del Sol

If you want to combine good food with an unbeatable location, dining with views over the square is an experience that is hard to match: enjoying Madrid cuisine while watching the bustle of kilometre zero.
