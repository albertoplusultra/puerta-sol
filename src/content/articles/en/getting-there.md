Getting to **Puerta del Sol** is very easy: it is Madrid's most central public transport hub and is perfectly connected to the whole city and region.

## By metro

**Sol metro station** is served by **lines 1 (blue), 2 (red) and 3 (yellow)**. It is one of the busiest stations on the network and connects with the rest of the lines via a couple of transfers.

## By commuter rail

Beneath the square is **Sol commuter rail station**, a key point on the suburban train network that crosses the centre and links with the main stations (Atocha, Chamartín, Nuevos Ministerios) and the whole Community of Madrid.

## By bus

Numerous **EMT** bus lines stop around the square. It's a good option for surface travel and for seeing the city along the way.

## On foot

Puerta del Sol is at the heart of the **pedestrian centre**. From squares and monuments such as Plaza Mayor, Gran Vía or the Royal Palace, it's a few minutes' walk, so many visitors prefer to discover the area on foot.

## From the airport

From **Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas Airport**, the most convenient public-transport option is to combine metro line 8 with a transfer towards the centre, or use commuter rail. The journey usually takes 40 to 50 minutes.

## Practical tip

Central Madrid is within a **low-emission zone** with restricted traffic, so avoid driving. Staying near the square lets you move around on foot and make the most of your visit.
