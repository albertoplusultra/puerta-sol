Puerta del Sol is the **best starting point** to discover Madrid's historic centre. From here, almost everything is comfortably reachable on foot. These are the must-see places just minutes from the square.

## Plaza Mayor

A five-minute walk away, **Plaza Mayor** is one of the most beautiful arcaded squares in Spain. Surrounded by porticoes, cafés and the equestrian statue of Philip III, it is a must.

## Mercado de San Miguel

Next to Plaza Mayor, **Mercado de San Miguel** is a gourmet market ideal for sampling tapas, cheeses, cured meats and wines in a lively atmosphere.

## Royal Palace and Almudena Cathedral

Just over ten minutes away are the **Royal Palace**, the largest royal residence in Western Europe by floor area, and **Almudena Cathedral**, overlooking the Campo del Moro gardens.

## Barrio de las Letras

To the east, the **Barrio de las Letras** preserves the flavour of literary Madrid from the Golden Age, with pedestrian streets, terraces and bookshops.

## Shopping streets and Gran Vía

From Sol begin the pedestrian streets of **Preciados** and **Carmen**, two of the busiest shopping arteries, leading up to **Gran Vía**, the epicentre of shops, theatres and cinemas.

With Puerta del Sol as your base, it is easy to plan a full day taking in the best of the centre without needing any transport.
