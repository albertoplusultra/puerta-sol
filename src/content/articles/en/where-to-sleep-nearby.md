Staying near **Puerta del Sol** means sleeping in the **exact centre of Madrid**. It is the preferred choice for those who want to make the most of their visit: almost everything is a few minutes' walk away and transport connections are unbeatable.

## Advantages of staying next to Sol

- **Everything at hand**: Plaza Mayor, the Royal Palace, Gran Vía and the Barrio de las Letras are minutes away on foot.
- **Perfect transport**: metro and commuter rail beneath the square to reach the whole city and region.
- **Historic-centre atmosphere**: experience the city day and night without relying on a car.
- **Ideal for New Year's Eve**: if you come for the chimes, staying nearby saves you long journeys.

## How to choose accommodation

Look for well-located accommodation, with good sound insulation if you're sensitive to noise, and rooms suited to your kind of trip. Booking early is key in high season and especially for New Year's dates.

## Accommodation right in the centre

For those seeking charming accommodation just a few metres from the square, sleeping in the historic centre lets you start each day right in the heart of Madrid.
