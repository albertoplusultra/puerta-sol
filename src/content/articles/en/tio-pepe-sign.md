The **Tío Pepe sign** is Madrid's most famous illuminated advertisement and one of the most recognisable images of Puerta del Sol. Its figure —a bottle dressed in a jacket, hat and guitar— has crowned the square's buildings for decades.

## An advertising icon turned heritage

The neon sign, promoting a well-known sherry brand, was installed in Puerta del Sol in the mid-**20th century**. Over time it stopped being seen as mere advertising and became a beloved element of Madrid's urban landscape.

## Relocations and return

During refurbishment works, the sign was temporarily removed, which prompted a notable public reaction. It was eventually **reinstalled** in the square, recognising its value as a hallmark of Puerta del Sol. Today it shines again over the rooftops, especially striking at dusk.

## Where to see it

The sign crowns one of the buildings at the junction of the square with **Calle de Alcalá**. It is easy to spot by looking up, and offers one of the most photographed night-time views of Madrid.
