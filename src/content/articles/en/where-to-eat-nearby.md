Eating near **Puerta del Sol** is one of the best ways to enjoy central Madrid. The area is packed with historic taverns, gourmet markets and restaurants for every taste, most just a few minutes' walk from the square.

## What to try

Madrid cuisine offers dishes full of character. The essentials include **cocido madrileño** (chickpea stew), **callos a la madrileña** (tripe), the classic **fried-squid sandwich** and, to finish, **churros with chocolate** or **torrijas** in season.

## Tapas in the centre

The area around Sol and neighbouring Plaza Mayor is ideal for **tapas**: small plates to share, local wines and a lively atmosphere. Mercado de San Miguel, a few minutes away, is a great spot for varied nibbling.

## Eating with views over Puerta del Sol

If you want to combine good food and an unbeatable location, dining with views over the square is a hard experience to match: enjoying Madrid cuisine while watching the bustle of Kilometre Zero.
