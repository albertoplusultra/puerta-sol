**Kilometre Zero** is one of the must-see stops in Puerta del Sol. It is a plaque set into the pavement, in front of the Real Casa de Correos, marking the exact point from which road distances in Spain are measured.

## What it represents

The **six radial roads** (A-1 to A-6) that connect Madrid with the rest of the country symbolically begin here. That is why it is considered, figuratively, the geographic centre of Spain for the road network.

## What the plaque looks like

The current granite and bronze plaque shows a schematic map of Spain with the radial roads and the inscription "Origin of the radial roads". Visitors often queue to photograph themselves with a foot on it: tradition says that whoever steps on it will return to Madrid.

## Where to find it

The plaque is on the ground, right in front of the façade of the **Casa de Correos**, seat of the Presidency of the Community of Madrid. As it is a discreet element set into the paving, look down so you don't miss it among the bustle.
